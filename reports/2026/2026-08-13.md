## Engineering changes

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): stamount_signed_add ROUNDS its operands, not truncates** (`6798100`, xrpl-validator)
  An STAmount operand is already canonical in rippled — it was rounded to 16 digits when it was produced — so reducing an over-precise operand by truncation drops exactly the digit that decides the result. `norm16` truncates.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): an INPUT-limited fill rounds the offer's output DOWN** (`ca17418`, xrpl-validator)
  BookStep.cpp:653, verbatim, with rippled's own rationale above it: ofrAmt = offer.limitIn(ofrAmt, inLmt, /* roundUp */ false);
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): measure a hop's carry at full precision, not by differencing** (`44c20d9`, xrpl-validator)
  The forward pass measured what a hop produced by differencing the sender's BALANCE. A balance holds 16 significant digits TOTAL, so whatever the sender already holds of the intermediate eats the low end of the addend — the hop is fine, the measurement is not.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): reprice a rested remainder in BOTH directions** (`afb133f`, xrpl-validator)
  The one-way clamp on the reprice — take `derived` only when it moves the residual DOWN — was a workaround for #105930662 40FB322EC16C from when the reprice still used the RAW ratio. `070ca31` fixed the rate itself and that ledger is byte-exact now, so the guard had outlived its cause and did nothing except suppress the reprice wherever it needed to move the residual UP.
