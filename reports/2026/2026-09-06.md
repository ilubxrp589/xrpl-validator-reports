## Engine findings fixed

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): F185 — an NFTokenCreateOffer with a past Expiration is tecEXPIRED** (`a8e8cde`, xrpl-validator)
  `hasExpired(ctx.view, ctx.tx[~sfExpiration])` — the parent ledger's close time at or past the offer's Expiration — is the FIRST test of NFTokenCreateOffer::preclaim (NFTokenCreateOffer.cpp), before the token is looked up. The native transactor had no expiration test and created the offer.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): F187 — a created DID object carries Flags: 0** (`0b4ea2a`, xrpl-validator)
  `sfFlags` is soeREQUIRED on the DID ledger format, so rippled serialises `Flags: 0` (`22 00000000`) right after the entry type of every DID it creates, whether or not DIDSet touches it. Our DIDSet built the object without the field.
