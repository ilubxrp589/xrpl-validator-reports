## Engineering changes

- **fix(peer): our validations get their own queue and go to every regular peer; peers' transactions are no longer relayed to the hubs** (`a6ab3ee`, xrpl-validator)
  data.xrpl.org counted 53 of 19,441 ledgers missed in a day, all of them signed. Two ways a signed validation was lost: - It shared a 2,000-place queue to the hubs with every transaction a peer sent us. A ledger of 1,000-2,000 transactions overflowed it, and a session that fell behind skipped the oldest frames, our validation among them (#107741340: four hubs connected, three public servers never saw it). - It went …
- **fix(validator): sign a ledger only once the trusted validators reach quorum on its hash** (`d540e6e`, xrpl-validator)
  Until now the validator signed the hash the first peer announced as accepted. On mainnet #107746977 and #107747014 (18 Oct) that peer had built another ledger, so we signed a hash the network never validated, and data.xrpl.org scored both ledgers missed.
