_Hand-written notes on the day's XRPL work: research and operations._

- **Soak #35: two engine receipts, both fixed; ended by the deployment below.** Soak #35 of build 0145757 began at
  17:58 EDT on 2026-09-28 (seed ledger 107,300,970) and ended with the deployment of 50a05ae at 10:58 EDT, after
  about 17 hours. It recorded two engine receipts, F407 at 04:46 EDT and F408 at 07:54 EDT, both below. Every check
  during it found no state-hash mismatch (the last at 09:17 EDT), with the validator on its primary reference node
  and no failovers: the receipts are differences in the native engine's own results, found by its comparison
  against libxrpl, not in the validator's state. From 08:26 EDT the comparison itself was out of step (below).
- **Finding F407 (a trust line whose only setting is the default quality).** At ledger 107,311,328 an account sent a
  TrustSet on a CNY trust line, setting the limit to 0, setting NoRipple and clearing freeze. Afterwards the line held no
  balance, the account's limit was 0, and NoRipple is that account's default (it does not enable DefaultRipple); the
  only other thing stored for its side was an incoming quality of 1,000,000,000. That value,
  QUALITY_ONE, is a rate of exactly 1, the same as no quality at all, and rippled's TrustSet treats a stored
  QUALITY_ONE as zero before deciding whether either side still has an interest in the line. Neither side did, so
  mainnet deleted the line: the line and the issuer's directory page went, the account's directory page shrank, and
  the account's owner count fell from 1 to 0. The engine counted any non-zero stored quality as an interest and kept
  the line. It now treats a stored QUALITY_ONE as no quality, as rippled does. The mainnet transaction is pinned as a
  regression vector. Fixed in e1140ef.
- **Finding F408 (AMM deposit: the minimum is not the amount issued).** At ledger 107,314,214 an account deposited
  1.021192 XRP into an XRP/token AMM pool as a single-asset deposit, naming 56,639.2869999 LP tokens in the
  transaction's LPTokenOut field. In a single-asset deposit that field is only a minimum: rippled issues the LP tokens
  the deposited amount buys at the pool's price, and refuses the deposit (tecAMM_FAILED) only when that is fewer than
  the minimum. The XRP bought 57,211.40098 tokens, and mainnet issued 57,211.40098. The engine moved the same XRP but
  issued exactly the minimum, about 572 tokens short. It now issues what the deposit buys and refuses a deposit that
  falls below its minimum. The same code decided a second kind of single-asset deposit, in which the account names the
  tokens it wants: rippled issues that request rounded down to the precision at which the pool's total can record it,
  and the engine now does too (it issued the unrounded request). The mainnet transaction is pinned as a regression
  vector; the refusal and the rounding, which no mainnet transaction has exercised yet, are covered by unit tests.
  Fixed in 1fd1b9b.
- **The native-engine comparison lost step at 08:26 EDT, and was repaired.** Before verifying a ledger with libxrpl,
  the validator checks that its stored state is aligned with that ledger: the sender of the ledger's first
  sequence-numbered transaction must hold that sequence number. At ledger 107,314,806 that sender's account flags (it blocks incoming trust lines and NFT
  offers, flags 0x24000000) begin with the same byte that marks the sequence field, and the check searched for that
  byte instead of reading the fields in order, so it read 36 where the account held 62,037,183. It concluded the
  state was out of step, declined to verify the ledger, and handed the native-engine comparison an empty result; the
  comparison, which resets its in-memory copy of the ledger to the libxrpl result after every ledger, lost that
  ledger's changes and from then on reported a difference on every ledger: 2,303 ledgers, 107,314,806 to
  107,317,138, until the deployment rebuilt its copy. None were engine errors and the validator was unaffected: its
  state hash matched the network on every ledger throughout. Of the flagged ledgers the automated re-check reached,
  65 matched mainnet on every transaction (7,398 of 7,398); the other two, 107,314,819 and 107,314,838, match on
  the full ledger state with the flow engine the validator runs (105 of 105 and 251 of 251), the re-check having
  used the engine's older flow code for the first and an incomplete pre-state for one failed payment in the second.
  Fixed in 50a05ae: the check reads the account's fields in order, and a ledger the libxrpl side did not verify now
  makes the comparison rebuild its copy from the validator's stored state instead of adopting an empty result. A
  replay against mainnet of one ledger in twenty from the affected range, with the flow engine the validator runs,
  matched on every transaction: 117 ledgers, 16,302 of 16,302 transactions (finished 16:17 EDT).
- **Deployment.** Build 50a05ae (F407, F408 and the fix above) replaced the live build at 10:58 EDT, after its gate
  (1,697 suite tests and 745 flow-engine tests passing) and both mainnet replay windows (60 and 98 ledgers, zero

- **Soak #36.** At 18:18 EDT, 6.9 hours in, soak #36 of build 50a05ae was healthy: 6,801 state-hash matches and no
