## Engineering changes

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): reprice bridged leg A after the mid-leg rounds up** (`07f2e32`, xrpl-validator)
  9bb5ceb rounded the bridged XRP mid-leg UP to whole drops and left `gets_in` priced from the FRACTIONAL value it had before. So leg A took a whole drop and paid for a fraction less of one, every time the rounding did anything.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a payment pays the INPUT issuer's TransferRate** (`c99ebb2`, xrpl-validator)
  `apply_path_payment` charged the OUTPUT issuer's rate (`want_rate`) and let the input through free. A payment sets `ownerPaysTransferFee_ = false`, so `trIn = transferRate(book_.in.account)`: the SENDER parts with `amount × rate` and the counterparty receives `amount` (BookStep.cpp:737-739). `apply_iou_direct` has always done this for the same-currency case — the crossing path never did.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): match rippled's AMM slice sizing on direct offer crossing** (`b2c47da`, xrpl-validator)
  Two coupled rules, both read off libxrpl's own trace of #106143011 4607B92B against the RLUSD/XRP pool and confirmed in rippled's source.
- **fix(xrpl-node): choose the hydration RPC per LEDGER, not per sweep script** (`25c9e31`, xrpl-validator)
  The gate corpora AGE OUT of our LAN validator's rolling window (~290k ledgers on 2026-08-10, 105919185-106209950), so a fixture that hydrated locally last month must come from s2 today. A server pinned per script cannot express that, and both failure directions were live: * `batch2sweep.sh` pins `--rpc <lan>` while 7 of its 26 ledgers had rolled out of the window (105909285 105909624 105910649 105911834 105912291 …
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): mint real LP tokens for AMMCreate and single-asset deposits** (`85bb7df`, xrpl-validator)
  Both mint sites were hardcoded 1e7 placeholders, documented as "the magnitude is oracle-corrected downstream". They were 23 of the 83 value divergences at gate 46 and by far the largest in the backlog — relative errors up to 3e5, where the whole remaining OfferCreate tail sits below 2e-4.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): quantize a deposit's LP mint to the pool balance's ulp** (`acf49ab`, xrpl-validator)
  SETTLES THE MANTISSA-SCALE QUESTION, which `85bb7df` left open as the suspected cause of the single-asset deposit precision tails. It is NOT the cause.
