  **BatchV1_1 still holds its majority** (since 2026-09-15 14:06 UTC) and activates around 2026-09-29 14:06 UTC.
- **The second clean 24-hour run.** The live soak started 2026-09-22 11:38 EDT passed its 24-hour mark with zero
  engine receipts — every compared ledger matched rippled byte-for-byte (the first such run was 2026-09-20/21).
- **Batch conformance ahead of activation.** Campaign 23 (devnet) crossed Batch with delegation, MPT,
  credentials, tickets, AccountDelete and LedgerStateFix inners: six engine rules fixed (F389–F394). The subtlest:
  rippled threads an owner's AccountRoot (`threadOwners`) only for nodes an inner itself inserted or erased, in that
  inner's own ApplyStateTable — not for whatever the batch as a whole created. Campaign 24 (devnet) put NFT, offer,
  check, payment-channel, escrow, oracle, DID, AMM, trust-line and AccountSet inners inside Batches, each shaped so
  a later inner uses what an earlier one created: 27/27 byte-exact on the first run.
- **An independent review against the rippled 3.4.0 sources** of the Batch fixes found two more issues, fixed
  test-first (F395, F396): a replay-only pre-image read of raw leaves, and inner transaction ids hashed without the
  codec's canonical spelling of decimal MPT amounts and short-hex `AssetPrice`.
- **Desk checks for the two Batch-only bug classes found so far:** no other transactor copies raw transaction
  `Flags` into a ledger object (where `tfInnerBatchTxn` would leak), and none gates the fee level in preclaim (a
  Batch inner carries `Fee: "0"`).
- **Deployment.** The engine build f7ad693 (findings through F396) replaced the live shadow build at 15:31 EDT after
  its gate (1,589 suite tests and 706 flow-engine tests passing) and two mainnet replay windows (60 and 98 ledgers,
  zero mismatches). The post-deployment health check matched 402 ledgers with no mismatches, and a new 24-hour soak
  began at 15:57 EDT (seed ledger 107,187,825).
- **Receipt canary — first live run.** One drop was planted in the shadow's copy of a mainnet AccountRoot at ledger
  107,188,304; the byte-for-byte compare flagged it and the receipt watcher logged it 255 seconds later. Canary lines
  are never counted as receipts: the check exists to prove that a zero-receipt soak means the comparison was running
  and sensitive. It now runs daily.
