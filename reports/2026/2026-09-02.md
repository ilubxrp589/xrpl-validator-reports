  CheckCreate::preclaim (rippled CheckCreate.cpp, ungated) rejects a non-XRP SendMax with tecFROZEN when the currency is globally frozen, when the issuer froze the source's trust line (the issuer's side bit), or when the destination froze its own line (the destination's side bit); a party without a line passes. - **fix(ledger): an in-limited bridged slice floors the XRP mid-leg for a book leg too (finding 93)** (`2610407`, xrpl-validator)
  The bridged walk rounded the XRP mid-leg up unless leg A was a pool that exhausted the taker's in (finding 51) or the leg-B maker's funds bound the slice (finding 62). rippled's forward pass is in-limited whenever the slice takes the taker's whole remaining in — `limitStepIn` → `ceilInStrict(…, roundUp = false)` → `divRoundStrict(limit, rate, XRP, false)` — for any offer type, so a BOOK leg A floors as well.

### Automated research — candidate amendment ideas

_Ideas generated and prior-art-checked by the XRPL Amendment Board, an automated research tool (read-only board: [iceland.halcyon-names.io](https://iceland.halcyon-names.io/)). They are exploration leads, not proposals._

- **Oracle-Bounded Concentrated AMM Liquidity** (amm-defi)
  Add a new AMMLiquidityPosition ledger object (owner-reserved, keylet::ammPosition(ammID, account, seq)) carrying sfTickLower/sfTickUpper price-tick SF
- **Shielded EscrowFinish for Confidential Transfers** (privacy)
  Add an lsfConfidential flag and sfAmountCommitment (a Pedersen commitment, reusing ConfidentialTransfer's commitment scheme) to Escrow objects created
- **Byte-Weighted Owner Reserves** (fees)
