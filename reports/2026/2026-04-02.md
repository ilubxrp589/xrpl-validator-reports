### Other engineering changes

- **perf: parallel object fetching — 32 concurrent RPC requests per ledger** (`2241766`, xrpl-validator)
  Replace sequential ledger_entry fetch loop with tokio semaphore-bounded parallel requests. 500 objects: 1.5s sequential → ~0.2s parallel.
- **perf: bump concurrent RPC fetches from 32 to 64** (`4dd589f`, xrpl-validator)
  Round time: 1.2-1.4s → 0.8-1.2s. Fetch overhead now ~0.1s. Bottleneck is pure hash computation (~1s), not network.
- **perf: depth-4 caching (65536 buckets) — hash compute 1s → 15ms** (`ef3a806`, xrpl-validator)
  Replace 256 depth-2 buckets with 65536 depth-4 buckets. Only ~500 of 65536 buckets are dirty per ledger (0.8%) vs 230 of 256 (90%) before.
