## Notes

_Hand-written notes on the day's XRPL work: research and operations._

- **Soak #32: two engine receipts, both fixed.** Soak #32 of build c79ee89 (from 13:21 EDT on 2026-09-26) recorded two
  engine receipts, F403 and F404 below; it ended with this morning's deployment at 08:01 EDT. Over the soak the
  shadow compared 16,823 ledgers and the state-hash check matched 16,828, with no state-hash mismatch: the receipts
  are differences in the native engine's own results, found by its comparison against libxrpl, not in the validator's
  state.
- **Finding F403 (Clawback of an issued token).** At ledger 107,263,299 an issuer clawed back 200,000 units of its
  token from a trust line holding 200,000.0000000005. rippled moves the smaller of the holder's balance and the amount
  with exact decimal arithmetic and left 0.0000000005 on the line; the engine did the subtraction in binary floating
  point, which cannot represent 200,000.0000000005, and wrote about 0.00000000049476. Clawback now moves the amount
  through the engine's exact trust-line credit, the one payments use, which also brings rippled's line bookkeeping.
  The mainnet transaction is pinned as a regression vector. Fixed in e7d7655.
- **Finding F404 (XRP payment paid for with a Ticket).** At ledger 107,266,300 an account holding 3.002177 XRP with
  ten tickets (owner count 10) sent 5,877 drops using one of them. rippled deletes the ticket being spent before it
  checks the payment against the reserve, so the reserve was 1 + 0.2 × 9 = 2.8 XRP and mainnet accepted the payment;
  the engine checked earlier, still counting the ticket (3.0 XRP), and refused it. The same pattern as F402
  (TicketCreate, 2026-09-25). Fixed in ae84a26. Two other transaction types, EscrowCreate and TrustSet, also check
  their reserve after the ticket is consumed in rippled while the engine checks before; no mainnet transaction has
  exercised the difference, and devnet transactions will be generated to test both.
- **Deployment.** Build ae84a26 replaced the live build at 08:01 EDT, after its gate (1,691 suite tests and 745
  flow-engine tests passing) and both mainnet replay windows (60 and 98 ledgers, zero mismatches), by warm restart:
  it rebuilt its state in 22.4 seconds and resumed two ledgers behind the network. The health check matched 410
  ledgers with no mismatches, and soak #33 began at 08:28 EDT (seed ledger 107,269,979). Besides F403 and F404 it
  adds two read-only status endpoints for the operations monitor (the validator's connections, and its amendment
  votes) and changes the votes.
- **Amendment votes.** The validator now votes for exactly the pending amendments its engine implements and has
  verified: PermissionDelegationV1_1 is added (implemented and verified against devnet), XChainBridge and
  fixXChainRewardRounding are removed (the engine's cross-chain bridge code has never run against a real bridge).
  45 votes instead of 46. The first voting ledger after the deployment, 107,270,143, carried the 45 votes and reached
  the reference node.
- **Amendment readiness.** The operations monitor now warns, with the date, when an amendment with majority on
