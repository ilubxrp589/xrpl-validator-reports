- **feat: full transaction execution, independent ledger close, RPCA consensus** (`d7fe7ae`, xrpl-validator)

- **fix: disable state write-back to prevent hit rate drift** (`f23c96b`, xrpl-validator)
  Partial execution (fees + XRP payments only) was modifying cached network state in RocksDB, causing cumulative balance mismatches. Track execution in-memory only until full tx execution is complete.
