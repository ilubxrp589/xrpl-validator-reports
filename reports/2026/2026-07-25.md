## Engineering changes

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): cap payment delivery at the destination's trust limit** (`04a1586`, xrpl-validator)
  The last TER-class divergence cluster (6: 105740164/105741244/105741859, 2 each) was not the two-hop AMM strand bug the handoff suspected. rippled's final delivery step DirectStepI::maxPaymentFlow (DirectStep.cpp:487) limits what a payment can land on the destination to creditLimit(dst,issuer) minus what it already holds, so a destination at or over its own trust limit receives nothing and the whole flow is …
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): cross an underfunded maker at book quality, don't rest** (`84898d4`, xrpl-validator)
  The section-1 achieved-quality break (offer.rs) was calibrated for the #105780948 IoC tecKILLED, where the TAKER's own side bounds the fill and the floored output realises worse than the limit. It also fired, wrongly, on a plain offer crossing a maker that is merely UNDERFUNDED at a book quality equal to the taker's limit: the <=1e-7 round-up on `pay` for the small maker-funds-clamped fill tips the achieved rate …
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): re-check a maker's actual quality, not just its book page** (`7e592e9`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled compares a crossing maker's ACTUAL getRate to the taker's limit, not merely the quantized book-directory level it rests in. An offer can sit in a page that ties the threshold while its exact rate is one ULP worse, and rippled leaves such an offer untouched.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): honour tfPassive -- don't consume equal-quality makers** (`77f6f39`, xrpl-validator)
  We never read tfPassive: a passive offer was crossed like a normal one. A passive offer crosses only STRICTLY better makers and rests behind equal-quality ones (rippled OfferCreate.cpp:396 `++threshold`). Our rate encoding is inverted (lower = better, monotone in the u64), so tighten the threshold by one ULP for passive offers; the existing q<=threshold and maker-rate gates then exclude the equal level.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): implement AMMWithdraw tfWithdrawAll teardown** (`a7bd6a0`, xrpl-validator)
  tfWithdrawAll fell through the partial-withdraw path: it withdrew NO assets (no Amount fields to loop over) and burned a fixed default LPToken amount, leaving the LPToken trust line Modified-at-zero instead of torn down.
- **fix(differential_probe): hydrate the issuer's owner-dir tail for CheckCash** (`7eb0dca`, xrpl-validator)
  CheckCash creates the delivered IOU's trust line, which the ISSUER co-owns -- but native_read_keys loaded only the casher's owner-dir root, so an issuer with a multi-page directory was never hydrated and dir_insert invented a fresh root instead of appending to the existing last page.
