## Engineering changes

- **fix(probe): a dropped fetch is not a divergence — withhold the verdict** (`cae6b85`, xrpl-validator)
  Both probes conflated "the network dropped this fetch" with "this object does not exist", so an incomplete pre-state replayed into a plausible, well-formed divergence. #106099077 is what prompted this: the scout filed two `tecPATH_DRY vs tesSUCCESS` circular-arb Payments on the FFI leg, with a triage note blaming the offer-book directory walk and `succ`/overlay merge. Re-running the same fixture on the same binary — …
- **fix(ledger): a hydrated maker with no line for what it sells holds ZERO** (`44d7a1c`, xrpl-validator)
  #106096771 354/354 (was 352/354) and #106101763 224/224 (was 223/224) — three divergences across two independent ledgers, one root cause. Both came from the 2026-08-06 fresh scout batch, and #106101763 was never looked at while making the change: the first evidence this run that a fix generalises rather than fitting the ledger it came from.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): auth is not an interest in a trust line** (`f3f8b98`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled's bLowReserveSet/bHighReserveSet (SetTrust.cpp:611-620) are exactly quality-in, quality-out, NoRipple-vs-DefaultRipple, freeze, limit and balance. We also tested lsfLowAuth/lsfHighAuth, so a single authorisation bit pinned a zero-balance, zero-limit line open forever.
- **feat(xrpl-ledger): DX_SEL — measure strand selection against rippled's model** (`dbb9110`, xrpl-validator)
  Settles what rippled actually does, and relocates the defect.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): the bridge strand's upper bound must not charge the transfer fee** (`005ad9a`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled states the rule outright in adjustQualityWithFees (BookStep.cpp): "It is important that `qualityUpperBound` is an upper bound on the quality (it is used to ignore strands whose quality cannot meet a minimum threshold). When calculating quality assume no fee is charged, or the estimate will no longer be an upper bound." It therefore returns ofrQ UNCHANGED for a CLOB tip and for a multi-path AMM, and even in …
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): strand selection — upper-bound order with fall-through** (`03c2cb9`, xrpl-validator)
  The judge and the fall-through are ONE mechanism, which is why building the judge alone failed twice (recorded 8 v 17: it rejects the leading candidate and then crosses nothing).
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a rejected candidate's offer removals must survive its rollback** (`a836f94`, xrpl-validator)
  Hunted for a ledger exercising the ofrsToRm deviation noted in 03c2cb9. There isn't one: DX_RM swept all 144 staged fixtures (batch2, newfinds, freshfinds, corpus) and reported ZERO cases where a rejected candidate's rollback discarded a reap. The one hit, #105912454, is the benign kind -- offers the fill CONSUMED, which rippled discards too, since it drops the failed strand's sandbox and carries out only ofrsToRm.
