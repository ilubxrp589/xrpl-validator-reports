- **feat(xrpl-node): stage3 prep — config, batch helpers, hybrid singleton model** (`9d9f694`, xrpl-validator)
  Adds the building blocks for Stage 3 (FFI bytes as the source of truth in state.rocks) WITHOUT wiring them into the live loop. The wiring is deliberately deferred until after Phase A — the 100K -> 200K -> 300K shadow-only battle test currently running on the validator host — clears with a clean shadow hash record. Flipping Stage 3 with even a small shadow mismatch rate would silently corrupt state.rocks for the divergent ledgers.
