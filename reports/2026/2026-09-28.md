- **Soak #33: one engine receipt, fixed.** Soak #33 of build ae84a26 ran from 08:28 EDT on 2026-09-27 to 08:32 EDT
  today and recorded one engine receipt, F405 below. Since that build's deployment at 08:01 EDT on 2026-09-27 the
  shadow compared 22,690 ledgers and the state-hash check matched 22,701, with no state-hash mismatch: the receipt is
  a difference in the native engine's own result, found by its comparison against libxrpl, not in the validator's
  state. The validator stayed on its primary reference node throughout, with no failovers.
- **Finding F405 (AMM withdrawal by the token's issuer).** At ledger 107,278,773 an account withdrew all of its share
  of an AMM pool of XRP and a token the account itself issues, while holding 1.41 XRP and owning two ledger objects.
  Before paying out a token, rippled checks that a withdrawer with no trust line for it could afford the reserve for
  a new one. The check asks only whether that trust line exists, and an issuer never has a trust line to itself, so
  it applies to the issuer too: a third object needs 1 + 0.2 × 3 = 1.6 XRP, and even with the pool's whole 0.1 XRP the
  account had at most 1.50999 XRP, so mainnet refused the withdrawal (tecINSUFFICIENT_RESERVE, with the fee charged).
  The engine exempted issuers from the check, an assumption made when the check was added on 2026-09-04 that no
  mainnet transaction had tested until this one, and paid out. The exemption is removed and the mainnet transaction
  is pinned as a regression vector. Fixed in 51b0cbe on 2026-09-27 and deployed today (below).
- **Deployment.** Build 21fdd93 replaced the live build at 10:09 EDT, after its gate (1,692 suite tests and 745
  flow-engine tests passing) and both mainnet replay windows (60 and 98 ledgers, zero mismatches), by warm restart:
  it rebuilt its state in 22.1 seconds and resumed two ledgers behind the network. The health check matched 401
  ledgers with no mismatches, and soak #34 began at 10:35 EDT (seed ledger 107,294,149). Besides F405 it fixes how the
  build links libxrpl's static libraries: they were linked in the order the filesystem listed them, which differs
  between machines, so the same link succeeded on one machine and failed on another. They are now linked in a fixed
  order, and gRPC, which nothing uses, is no longer linked.
- **Soak #34: one engine receipt, fixed.** Soak #34 of build 21fdd93 began at 10:35 EDT and ended about seven hours
  later with the deployment below. It recorded one engine receipt, F406. Every check during it found no state-hash
  mismatch (the last at 15:17 EDT); the receipt is a difference in the native engine's own result, found by its
  comparison against libxrpl, not in the validator's state.
- **Finding F406 (AMM withdrawal: which side is paid first).** At ledger 107,299,319 an account holding 9.381031 XRP
  and owning 41 ledger objects withdrew its share of an XRP/token pool by redeeming LP tokens, with no trust line for
  the token. The transaction named the token first and XRP second, but the pool's ledger object stores XRP first, and
  when a withdrawal names no amounts rippled pays the two sides in the object's order. So the XRP arrived first and
  counted toward the 9.4 XRP reserve the new trust line needed, and mainnet accepted the withdrawal. The engine paid
  the sides in the transaction's order, checked the token side against 9.381031 XRP and refused it
  (tecINSUFFICIENT_RESERVE). It now pays proportional withdrawals in the pool object's order, as its deposit code
  already did. The mainnet transaction is pinned as a regression vector. Fixed in 0145757.
- **Deployment.** Build 0145757 replaced the live build at 17:31 EDT, after its gate (1,693 suite tests and 745
  flow-engine tests passing) and both mainnet replay windows (60 and 98 ledgers, zero mismatches), by warm restart:
  it rebuilt its state in 22.6 seconds and resumed two ledgers behind the network. The health check matched 418
  ledgers with no mismatches, and soak #35 began at 17:58 EDT (seed ledger 107,300,970). It was the first deployment
  run from the operations team's version-controlled deployment scripts.
