- **fix(trust): a QualityIn of QUALITY_ONE is stored, only QualityOut folds to 0** (`b33685d`, xrpl-validator)
  TrustSet.cpp:337-341 folds QUALITY_ONE to 0 for QualityOut only; uQualityIn keeps it, so the existing-line set path and trustCreate store a QualityIn of 1e9 on the sender's side, and a zero-limit new line that sets it is not tecNO_LINE_REDUNDANT. We treated both fields alike and cleared it.
- **fix(the validator's ledger feed): the FFI leg skips pseudo-txs from peers too** (`c7d3f67`, xrpl-validator)
  Two FFI-leg divergences on the live validator (cycle 190), the first UNLModify ever: #108012288 (disable xrp.unic.ac.cy) and #108020224 (re-enable jon-nilsen.no), both tefFAILURE where the network had tesSUCCESS. Signing was untouched: the native engine rotates the NegativeUNL at each flag ledger and our own hash matched both.
- **fix(amm): AMMCreate checks auth, freeze and DefaultRipple before funds** (`cccdec7`, xrpl-validator)
  #108008726 34C1C80B45F6: a PLX/BEEP AMMCreate by an account whose BEEP line its issuer froze (lsfLowFreeze, the issuer being the low side). rippled's preclaim runs requireAuth on both amounts, then checkFrozen on both, then terNO_RIPPLE for an issuer without lsfDefaultRipple, all before the reserve and funds (AMMCreate.cpp:107-146). We had none of the three, so the frozen side counted as zero funds and we answered …
- **feat(writer): a hash failure patches the mirror in place; the breaker is an alarm only** (`3b4b64c`, xrpl-validator)
  #107989363 (10-29): our bytes failed the state hash, the writer retried from the network's bytes, and the mirror was dropped and rebuilt from state.rocks: 246 s, and with 5a's breaker off (cycle 190) every ledger in them went unsigned, 40 in all.
- **fix(ledger): deleting a legacy SignerList gives back 2 + its entries** (`b57328d`, xrpl-validator)
  #107989363 0A9A6F552C51 (SignerListSet, SignerQuorum 0) deleted a 2-entry list made before MultiSignReserve: no lsfOneOwnerCount, so rippled's removeSignersFromLedger gives back 2 + entries. Mainnet's OwnerCount went 14 -> 10; ours went 14 -> 13, because the delete branch always gave back one.
