### Other engineering changes

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): honour the NFT offer opt-out on Owner and Destination** (`8b23677`, xrpl-validator)
  An account can opt out of receiving NFT offers with `lsfDisallowIncomingNFTokenOffer` (0x04000000), and rippled's shared `tokenOfferCreatePreclaim` (NFTokenHelpers.cpp:824) honours that for BOTH accounts an offer names: a `Destination` must exist (tecNO_DST), and neither it nor the token's `Owner` may carry the flag -- either is tecNO_PERMISSION. We checked neither.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): finish CheckCreate's destination checks** (`edf4462`, xrpl-validator)
  Our CheckCreate preclaim stopped at "the destination exists". rippled's goes on to ask whether that destination will actually accept the check, and #105846674 F6CC9A594B17 is the case we were missing: a VRTY airdrop check to rnabZzjg, whose flags 0x120000 carry lsfRequireDestTag, sent with no DestinationTag. Mainnet claims the fee in one mutation; we created the Check and both directory entries in seven.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): cap a direct IOU payment at what the destination can receive** (`cce3c8d`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled caps a payment's final issuer->dest step at `creditLimit(dst,issuer) - heldByDst`, floored at zero (DirectStepI::maxPaymentFlow, DirectStep.cpp:487), so a destination already at or over its own trust limit receives nothing and the strand is dry. `apply_path_payment` has carried that cap since 04a1586; that commit called the direct route the same rule "in principle" and left it alone for want of a failing …
- **feat(xrpl-ledger): token escrow, and give escrows their directory entries** (`5704d68`, xrpl-validator)
  #105823810 6AB38288 escrows 3750000 STSH. We rejected it temBAD_AMOUNT and applied nothing (0v6); mainnet built it in 7 nodes. This is the escrow lead the handoff has carried for weeks -- it finally has a fixture.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): an AMM deposit must fund its XRP side after reserve** (`11a9c6c`, xrpl-validator)
  #105893158 85C32164 (7v1). We had only the reserve guard from 385e129, which asks whether the depositor clears its reserve at all. rippled additionally checks that the XRP side is FUNDABLE, and measures that against the reserve the depositor will owe AFTERWARDS: `xrpLiquid` is taken with the owner count bumped by one when there is no LPToken line yet, because the deposit is about to open one (AMMDeposit.cpp:230-244, …
- **fix(differential_probe): put the RPC server in the cache key** (`52a567d`, xrpl-validator)
  The dxcache is one directory shared by every probe run on the box, and they do not all talk to the same node. The nightly scout hydrates from our own the reference node (`runner.sh diff <seq> http://[host]`); re-probing an older find has to use s2, because the reference node's ~17k-ledger window rolled past it long ago. The key was method+params only, so one server's answers were served for the other server's question.
