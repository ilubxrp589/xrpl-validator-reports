## Engineering changes

- **feat(ffi,ledger): track 1 — arithmetic oracle exports, differential fuzz harness, two exact primitives** (`639e778`, xrpl-validator)
  The shim gains ffi/xrpl_arith.cpp: one C export per STAmount operation (mulRound, mulRoundStrict, divRound, divRoundStrict, multiply, divide, add, sub, canonicalize), getRate, and Number ops under the four rounding modes — pure functions of their arguments, no ledger. The shim build now matches rippled 3.3.0 (C++23 for std::expected; mpt-crypto from conan for Protocol.h's <mpt_protocol.h>).
- **feat(ledger): AMM drops conversion through Number::to_drops; fuzz STAmount multiply/divide and Number→drops** (`94e6904`, xrpl-validator)
  The shim exports Number's explicit int64 conversion (XRPAmount{Number}, `operator rep()`: fraction digits through the guard, the mode decides the drop) and the fuzz puts it, STAmount `multiply` (a Number product at nearest) and STAmount `divide` (the legacy `muldiv(num, 1e17, den) + 5` quotient) through libxrpl and the engine: 31 rows × 20,000 cases, zero mismatches.
- **feat(ffi,node): Batch leg A — apply inner transactions inside the outer's shim apply; skip inner ledger entries in the FFI feed** (`06b21f8`, xrpl-validator)
- **feat(node): count inner Batch entries as covered — stats field, metrics line, parity_probe completeness** (`964a862`, xrpl-validator)
  Eight of the eleven devnet Batch ledgers 5309212–5309670 probe CLEAN through the outer with every inner folded; the three DIVERGENT ones fail on VaultDeposit / LoanPay hydration (SingleAssetVault / LendingProtocol, devnet-only), not on Batch.
