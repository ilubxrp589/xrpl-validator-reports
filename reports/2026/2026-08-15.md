### Other engineering changes

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): the AMM anchors only on a tip the taker could actually trade** (`1a96950`, xrpl-validator)
  `tryAMM` is handed `offers.tip().quality()`, and a tip beyond the taker's limit is not a tip the offer stream ever reaches — rippled then calls it with NO clob quality, which is `maxOffer`, and `limitOut` trims that to the limit instead. Our direct-walk AMM turn ran BEFORE the `q > threshold` break and anchored the slice to a level the taker had already rejected, sizing the fill off a price that was never available.
- **fix(harness): hydrate the NFT offer CREATOR's pages, not just the token's owner** (`fbb4f96`, xrpl-validator)
  `load_nft_pages_for_tx` took `Owner` OR `Account` for NFTokenCreateOffer. On a BUY offer `Owner` is the token's current holder — the seller — so the creator was skipped, and the creator is precisely the party who will RECEIVE the token.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): ceilOut CLAMPS the input to the offer's own, and either side at zero consumes it** (`853e75a`, xrpl-validator)
  Two symptoms, one missing rule. `ceilOutImpl` ends with a step that is explicit in rippled's source, not a rounding artefact (Quality.cpp): Amounts result(MulRoundFunc(limit, quality.rate(), amount.in.asset(), roundUp), limit); // Clamp in if (result.in > amount.in) result.in = amount.in;
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): AccountDelete removes the owner DIRECTORY, not just the account** (`b301e07`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled's teardown erases the directory explicitly, before the account (AccountDelete.cpp): Keylet const ownerDirKeylet{keylet::ownerDir(accountID_)}; … if (view().exists(ownerDirKeylet) && !view().emptyDirDelete(ownerDirKeylet)) view().update(dst); view().erase(src);
- **feat(xrpl-ledger): DepositAuth — a payment needs the destination's permission** (`51300d1`, xrpl-validator)
  We had NO DepositAuth support at all: before this the only occurrence of the term in the crate was a comment about which flags an AMM account carries. So an account that had explicitly closed itself to unsolicited payments received them from us anyway.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): AMMWithdraw splits "no LP tokens" from "too few" — and the pool's own lines get hydrated** (`e982148`, xrpl-validator)
  `AMMWithdraw::preclaim` (AMMWithdraw.cpp:272-288) distinguishes two failures we collapsed into one: if (lpTokens <= beast::kZero) return tecAMM_BALANCE; // holding NONE if (*lpTokensWithdraw > lpTokens) return tecAMM_INVALID_TOKENS; // holding TOO FEW
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a tfSell FillOrKill completes on its FUNDED input, not on TakerGets** (`91cdc47`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled calls `flow(..., partialPayment = !tfFillOrKill, ...)`, so a FillOrKill offer either completes or moves nothing — and completion is the OUT side for a BUY (TakerPays delivered in full) but the IN side for a SELL.
