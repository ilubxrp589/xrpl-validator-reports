- **Soak #31: clean.** Soak #31 of build 0c76af1 (F402, the voting-ledger validation order and the vote change,
  deployed 2026-09-25 10:53 EDT) began at 11:20 EDT on 2026-09-25 (seed ledger 107,228,273) and ended at 11:25 EDT
  today with no engine receipts. The shadow compared 22,125 ledgers with no byte, result or overlay difference, and the
  state-hash check matched 22,278 consecutive ledgers (107,228,745 to 107,251,022) with no mismatch. The daily receipt
  canary, run at 12:40 EDT on 2026-09-25, was detected, so the zero count comes from a live comparison.
- **Deployment (validator connections).** Build c79ee89 replaced the live build at 12:54 EDT after its gate (1,685
  suite tests and 744 flow-engine tests passing; the flow-engine run now includes campaign 25's vectors) and both
  mainnet replay windows (60 and 98 ledgers, zero mismatches), by warm restart: it rebuilt its state in 22.4 seconds and resumed one ledger behind the network. Its two
  changes concern how the validator connects, not the engine:
  - The ledger subscription now returns to the reference node on its own. When the reference node cannot be reached the
    validator moves to a public server, and until now it stayed there until the next restart (on 2026-09-25 a warm
    restart moved it back). While on a public server it now checks the reference node once a minute and returns as
    soon as that node holds the network's latest validated ledger.
  - The validation relay replaced its fourth hub, which no longer accepts peer connections. Of the hubs tested, three
    of Ripple's accept our handshake; two community hubs answered that they were full, and one rejected the handshake.
    The fourth relay now alternates between the two full hubs, and after the deployment it was accepted on its third
    attempt. A failed attempt is logged with its reason and retried after 10 seconds, doubling to at most 10 minutes,
    instead of every 25 seconds.

  The health check matched 405 ledgers with no mismatches, and soak #32 began at 13:21 EDT (seed ledger 107,252,372).
- **Reference node stall and RPC failover.** At 03:16 EDT the reference node stopped answering requests for about four
  minutes. On the same host, an unrelated maintenance command (a text search across a large directory tree) saturated
  the system disk, the kernel moved about 5 GB of process memory to swap on that same disk, and the node stalled until
  the search ended. The validator's RPC client switched to a public fallback server twice (for 10.5 and 316 seconds)
  and returned to the reference node on its own. Its ledger-by-ledger verification paused after ledger 107,243,458 and
  resumed with 107,243,459: no ledger was skipped and none mismatched. The engine was not involved. Searches of that
  kind no longer run on that host.
- **Watchdog paging.** The ops copilot re-sent a warning every 15 minutes for as long as it stayed open, so a
  store-pruning warning that could not clear for days was sent 21 times in five hours. A warning is now sent once per
  episode, and again only when a new signal joins it; critical alerts still repeat after a cooldown (copilot commit
  4233ece). The warning itself overstated the risk: it projected the fill rate from the last 24 hours, which still held
  the one-off writes that followed the 2026-09-25 rotation and upgrade (19.6 GB a day). Over the following day the node
  used about 15 GB a day (10 to 19 GB a day from hour to hour), which leaves about 40 to 50 GB free when the next
  rotation runs (ledger 107,436,647, about 2026-10-04), against 58 GB at the end of the previous cycle. An earlier
  version of this note gave 11.6 to 13.2 GB a day and 65 to 75 GB; those figures came from overnight hours only.
- **fixBatchV1_2.** Its source is still not published: at 13:17 UTC there was no rippled 3.4.1 tag or release, and
  neither `develop` nor the 3.4 release and staging branches define the amendment. It keeps majority and activates
