  NFTokenBurn::preclaim lets a token's owner burn it unconditionally; any other account needs the token flagged burnable (0x0001 in the NFTokenID's flag word) and must be its issuer or the issuer's current NFTokenMinter — an issuer with no minter set fails that comparison, an issuer whose root is gone skips it (NFTokenBurn.cpp preclaim).
