### Other engineering changes

- **feat(xrpl-node): DX_VALCHECK compares EXACTLY, at STAmount precision** (`adca53a`, xrpl-validator)
  The target is byte parity: a validator serialises the stored decimal into the SLE and hashes it, so one digit apart is a different state hash and a diverged node however small the number. The old check could not certify that — it parsed both sides into f64 and compared against a 1e-12 tolerance.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): Number division DISCARDS its remainder at the 16-digit scale** (`e7183b3`, xrpl-validator)
  129 -> 74 byte-parity hits, 43% of the backlog, from one line. Payment 89 -> 42, OfferCreate 29 -> 21, no key-level regressions.

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): store offer residuals at STAmount precision, not exactly** (`4556384`, xrpl-validator)
  `TOffer::consume` subtracts through STAmount, so a maker's remaining side comes back rounded to 16 significant digits (half-even, via `Number` under fixUniversalNumber). `me_sub` is EXACT — it keeps every digit — so whenever `gives0 - give` needed more than 16 digits we stored precision the ledger cannot hold and rippled does not have. - **fix(xrpl-ledger): charge the input transfer fee once — the walk owns it** (`41ef4ac`, xrpl-validator)
  `payment.rs` and `offer.rs` both quoted BookStep.cpp:770 and both applied the issuer's rate, and neither comment knew the other existed. `measure_hop` GRANTS a balance and differences it, and the walk debits the fee per fill, so the fee was already inside `consumed` before the reverse pass grossed it up again — the rate compounded.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): price bridged fills at the filed BookDirectory rate** (`e2a9c99`, xrpl-validator)
  An offer carries two rates and they are not the same number: the rate it is FILED under (low 64 bits of `BookDirectory`, 16-digit mantissa, fixed at creation) and its current `TakerPays / TakerGets`, which drifts every time a residual is re-rounded. rippled prices a PARTIAL fill at the filed rate and a FULL one at the offer's own amounts.
