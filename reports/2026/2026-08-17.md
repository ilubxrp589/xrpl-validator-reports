- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a terminal issuer-only change ripples between gateways, never a book** (`04d6c8b`, xrpl-validator)
  toStrand appends a terminal order book for the delivered asset only when the CURRENCY changes (PaySteps.cpp:220-233); its own comment is explicit — "for offer crossing (only) we do use an offer book even if all that is changing is the Issue.account". A PAYMENT whose last hop already holds the delivered currency under a DIFFERENT issuer therefore gets no book at all: the delivery issuer enters normPath as an ACCOUNT …
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): the balance clamps the GROSS, and a pass that misses limitQuality is discarded whole** (`23f1153`, xrpl-validator)
  Two coupled rules behind an IoC cluster one bot repeated every ledger — 11 ledgers, 24 divergences, 77% of everything open. Neither rule fixes it alone.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a pseudo-account cannot receive an escrow** (`8e48764`, xrpl-validator)
  `EscrowCreate::preclaim` refuses a pseudo-account destination with tecNO_PERMISSION immediately after the tecNO_DST read, BEFORE the token helper and before the tag test (EscrowCreate.cpp:350-352). It is deliberately not amendment-gated, because every write to a discriminator field is.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): the strand upper bound charges the intermediate gateway's cut** (`6fb6cf7`, xrpl-validator)
  A payment book step composes its quality with `trIn` — the transfer rate of the book's IN currency when the previous step redeems — in `BookPaymentStep::adjustQualityWithFees` (BookStep.cpp:338-360), and that is what feeds `qualityUpperBound`. Offer crossing deliberately WAIVES the fee ("assume no fee is charged, or the estimate will no longer be an upper bound", BookStep.cpp:519-524); a PAYMENT does not. …
- **feat(probe): DX_FILL on the bridged legs, and lift the saturating key cap** (`8ef6ea5`, xrpl-validator)
  Two instruments, both inert unless their env var is set.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): the bridged walk never crossed an expired offer either** (`2aa1274`, xrpl-validator)
  `live_head` — the stepper the BRIDGED walk advances with — did not test `Expiration`.
