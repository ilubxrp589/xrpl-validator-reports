### Other engineering changes

- **fix(probe): differential_probe keeps a tecEXPIRED's erasures, as the node does (devnet #5533675 7E7F5D06640B)** (`59a0ddc`, xrpl-validator)
  The probe applies through its own copy of native_apply_one, and that copy restored the post-common snapshot on every claimed tec but tecKILLED. The node's copy settles a tecEXPIRED instead (finding 245: apply::settle_expired keeps the NFTokenOffer / Credential erasures and their directory and OwnerCount moves). So a Payment naming an expired credential (campaign 23, devnet #5533675) kept the credential the network …
- **fix(ffi): leg A credits a shared Batch inner to the outer its ParentBatchID names, not the last carrier** (`863651f`, xrpl-validator)
  F417's other half. Several outers can carry the very same inner and only one files it (mainnet #107541023: four outers wrapping one ticketed pair, the first discarded, the third filing the pair, the fourth after it). The native side pairs inner verdicts by ParentBatchID since b29d83a; leg A (ffi_engine's C++ compare) still filled `inner_of` in ledger order, so a shared inner's expected mutations folded into the LAST …
- **fix(flow): rippled 3.4.1's overflow hardening — checked savedIns/savedOuts sums in flow() and BookStep** (`3a9c669`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled 3.4.1 (unconditional, no amendment) folds every strand-level and book-level aggregate through checkedStepAddOpt: XRP (and MPT) add in checked int64, IOU through Number as before.
- **fix(ledger): fixBatchV1_2 — a Batch inner must be a RawTransaction object (rippled 3.4.1)** (`5710fd4`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled 3.4.1's one gated change (Batch.cpp:293): with fixBatchV1_2, every RawTransactions entry must be wrapped as RawTransaction, or the batch is temMALFORMED. The check is FIRST in the per-inner loop, ahead of the uniqueness check. fixBatchV1_2 was ON on mainnet by #107540484 (2026-10-09), ahead of BatchV1_1, so no mainnet ledger ran Batch without it; devnet and testnet did.
