- **fix(xrpl-ledger): F267 — brokered NFT accept must cover the ask after the broker fee (tecINSUFFICIENT_PAYMENT)** (`e094350`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled 3.3.0 NFTokenAcceptOffer::preclaim, brokered mode (:95-142): same token, same asset, no owner loop, so.Amount > bo.Amount -> tecINSUFFICIENT_PAYMENT; then, with an explicit NFTokenBrokerFee, the fee must be the buy asset, below the buy amount, and the ask must still be covered after it. Our preclaim had none of the amount checks.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): F268 — an out-limited leg-B book partial on the XRP bridge ceils the mid-leg strictly** (`123e9a7`, xrpl-validator)
