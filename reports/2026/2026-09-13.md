- **fix(xrpl-ledger): F267 — brokered NFT accept must cover the ask after the broker fee (tecINSUFFICIENT_PAYMENT)** (`e094350`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled 3.3.0 NFTokenAcceptOffer::preclaim, brokered mode (:95-142): same token, same asset, no owner loop, so.Amount > bo.Amount -> tecINSUFFICIENT_PAYMENT; then, with an explicit NFTokenBrokerFee, the fee must be the buy asset, below the buy amount, and the ask must still be covered after it. - **fix(xrpl-ledger): F276 — an offer rate whose quotient reaches 2^63 wraps through Number's signed mantissa** (`03b96c8`, xrpl-validator)
