## Engineering changes

- **fix(nftoken): decode the base58 Issuer field on authorised-minter mints** (`881e160`, xrpl-validator)
  NFTokenMint's decode_account_id was hex-only, but the probe does not hex-normalise the Issuer field (it is absent from ACCOUNT_FIELDS), so an authorised-minter mint's base58 Issuer silently failed to decode and fell back to tx.account -- the minter. One wrong account cascaded two ways: MintedNFTokens was credited to the minter instead of the issuer, and the NFTokenID embedded the minter, so the token's low-96 -- …
- **fix(clawback): decode the base58 holder from Amount.issuer** (`f6ed033`, xrpl-validator)
  Clawback resolves the token holder from the nested Amount.issuer field, but misc.rs::decode_account_id was hex-only. The probe hex-normalises only top-level ACCOUNT_FIELDS, never a nested amount object, so Amount.issuer arrives as a base58 r-address -- decoding it hex-only returned None and the transactor rejected a valid clawback as Malformed.
