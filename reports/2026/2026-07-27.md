- **fix(xrpl-ledger): step past a satisfied fill, and reap dust-backed offers** (`130c246`, xrpl-validator)
  Two more of `OfferStream::step`'s removal semantics, both needed to close #105778999 6B2A11B3 (5v15, missing 10, extra 0) -- a circular self-pay, SendMax 140 XRP into POSAA, tfPartialPayment.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): carry the trailing reap across book levels** (`ba73d4f`, xrpl-validator)
  The trailing step added in 130c246 stopped at the end of the page chain of the level it had been crossing on. rippled's stream spans the whole BOOK: `OfferStream::step` carries no quality test at all, so after a consumed offer it keeps advancing — and reaping — across quality levels until it reaches a LIVE offer, and only then does `execOffer`'s `checkQualityThreshold` return false and end the walk …
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a crossing that exhausts the account rests no remainder** (`bb522bf`, xrpl-validator)
  "If offer crossing exhausted the account's funds don't create the offer" (OfferCreate.cpp:479-484). This is the companion to the pre-crossing `accountFunds` clamp from 1de6d87: that one bounds what may be SPENT, this one bounds what may REST.

- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a bridged pass needs its book composition within the limit** (`625399c`, xrpl-validator)
  #105807256 84FD7DC8, the last divergence in the harness. A tfSell|tfPassive OfferCreate selling 3105.662739 BBRL for 607.478737 RLUSD: mainnet rests it in 4 nodes, we crossed 8 objects (12v4, all extra, nothing missing).
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a frozen holder can fund nothing** (`1c46cda`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled reads spendable IOU through `accountHolds` with `fhZERO_IF_FROZEN`, so a holder whose line the issuer has frozen has NO funds at all, however large the balance. `isFrozen` (RippleStateHelpers.cpp:127) is the issuer's GLOBAL freeze, or the ISSUER's side of the line -- `(issuer > account) ? lsfHighFreeze : lsfLowFreeze`. Our `available()` read the balance and nothing else, so a frozen holder could trade freely.
