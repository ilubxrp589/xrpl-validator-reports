- **feat(xrpl-ledger): tfSell crossing semantics — sell the whole TakerGets — corpus 96.3%** (`3a3f337`, xrpl-validator)
  A tfSell offer sells its ENTIRE TakerGets even if that acquires more than TakerPays; TakerPays is only a minimum. Our crossing engine capped each fill at rem_pays (the wanted amount) and terminated the walk once it was reached, so a tfSell that could fully sell only by taking the surplus was left with an unsold remainder — and a FillOrKill/ImmediateOrCancel sell was then wrongly killed. - **fix(xrpl-ledger): sell-side offer funding check — tecUNFUNDED_OFFER — corpus 96.8%** (`870eefd`, xrpl-validator)
  An OfferCreate can only sell what the account actually holds of the TakerGets asset. Our crossing engine checked the MAKER funding but never the taker: after the tfSell fix started crossing the full TakerGets, five arb offers from one account selling IOUs it holds NONE of (CLARITY, XALPHA) crossed and succeeded, where mainnet returns tecUNFUNDED_OFFER fee-only. Verified: the account CLARITY line was absent at seq-1.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): faithful OfferCreate preclaim funding — tecUNFUNDED_OFFER — corpus 96.8%** (`5b1d386`, xrpl-validator)
  OfferCreate::preclaim returned the generic TxResult::Unfunded (tecUNFUNDED) and rejected whenever balance < the FULL sell amount. Both are wrong: rippled unfunds only when accountFunds(TakerGets) <= 0 (for XRP: balance minus reserve — partially funded offers still cross) and the code is tecUNFUNDED_OFFER. The old check was also XRP-only.
