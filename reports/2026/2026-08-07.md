## Engineering changes

- **feat(xrpl-ledger): DX_ULP — the 1-ulp upper bound is unobservable, and the note was backwards** (`f2d9d9a`, xrpl-validator)
  Hunted for a ledger where the composed upper bound's last digit decides an admission, per the open item in 005ad9a. There isn't one.
- **fix(xrpl-node): the FFI oracle replayed every old fixture under TODAY'S rules** (`a191b79`, xrpl-validator)
  Re-minting the stale freshfinds/105663160 fixture (it lacked `tx_json` and had been skipped by every gate since 24 July) surfaced this.
- **fix(xrpl-node): prefetch the NFT offer's OWNER — the accept names it by hash** (`60fb653`, xrpl-validator)
  Root cause of #105663160 `5CCAAE138F560F07`, and it is OURS, not rippled's.
- **refactor(xrpl-ledger): remove DX_SEL — it now compares an abandoned model** (`17a99ef`, xrpl-validator)
  DX_SEL was added in dbb9110 to find a ledger where our estimate-based strand selection differed from rippled's upper-bound one. It found the answer, the answer was acted on, and the detector outlived its premise: since 03c2cb9 the engine SELECTS by that upper-bound model (`order`), so DX_SEL was comparing an abandoned model against the live one and reporting "mismatches" that described nothing real.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): destination checks come before the funding check** (`5626357`, xrpl-validator)
  #106136429 `6FF692D40C4F`, from the 2026-08-07 fresh batch. The FFI leg was clean, so the pre-state was sound and the engine was wrong.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): a payment crosses the payer's own offer** (`8d8ca2d`, xrpl-validator)
  #106137720 `F2C989D4843A`, from the 2026-08-07 fresh batch. FFI leg clean, so the pre-state was sound and the engine was wrong.
- **feat(xrpl-ledger): max_offer — rippled's single-path AMM slice, trace-exact** (`a171405`, xrpl-validator)
  Groundwork for the AMM bridge slice-sizing family, with the two specimens from the 2026-08-07 batch. NOT wired in; see below.
