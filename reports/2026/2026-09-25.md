- **fix(ledger): F401 — AMM withdrawal reserve check counts XRP already paid out (soak #29 receipt)** (`166f033`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled's AMMWithdraw::withdraw checks the reserve for a new trust line against max(priorBalance, balance) (AMMWithdraw.cpp:705), where balance is the account's live balance. It pays the transaction's Asset before it checks Asset2, so when Asset is XRP the check sees the XRP already withdrawn. withdraw_reserve_ok compared the pre-fee balance alone, which is exact for a single-asset withdrawal but not for a two-asset …
- **fix(ledger): F402 — TicketCreate paid with a Ticket does not count the ticket it spends** (`e63a7d9`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled's Transactor::apply runs consumeSeqProxy before doApply: a transaction paid for with a Ticket deletes that ticket first, lowering OwnerCount and TicketCount by one. TicketCreate's checks see the account after that: doApply requires the pre-fee balance to cover accountReserve(OwnerCount + TicketCount), and preclaim refuses with tecDIR_FULL only when curTicketCount + addedTickets - consumedTickets exceeds 250. …

## Notes

_Hand-written notes on the day's XRPL work: research and operations._

- **Deployment and the first warm restart.** Build 166f033 (findings F401, the voting-ledger validation order and the
  validation relay, all described below) replaced the live build at 09:46 EDT after its gate (1,636 suite tests and 707
  flow-engine tests passing) and both mainnet replay windows (60 and 98 ledgers, zero mismatches). It was the first
  deployment to use the warm restart merged on 2026-09-24: the stopping build recorded its last verified ledger, and the
  new build rebuilt its stored state in 22.1 seconds, confirmed that the state's root equals the network's account hash
  for ledger 107,227,250, and resumed two ledgers behind the network. Cold starts had resumed 90–111 ledgers behind,
  several minutes after the switch. The health check matched 400 ledgers with no mismatches; soak #30 began at 10:14 EDT.
- **Voting-ledger validations.** Every 256th ledger (sequence ≡ 255 mod 256) is a voting ledger: a validator's
  validation of it carries its amendment votes. The node signed those validations but placed the signature after the
  amendment list, which puts the fields out of canonical order, and rippled drops a validation whose fields are out of
  order (the check is present in rippled 3.3.0 and 3.4.0). The reference node's record from 2026-09-24 12:48 to
  2026-09-25 00:22 EDT has our validation for none of the 41 voting ledgers in that period, so the network never
  received our amendment votes; it has our validation for every other ledger after 21:00 on 2026-09-24. The signature
  now takes its canonical place; the signed content is unchanged. Before deployment this was verified on mainnet with a
  throwaway key: the same voting-ledger validation, sent through a public hub, reached the reference node 93 ms later in
  the corrected layout and did not arrive in the old one. After deployment the reference node received our validation
  of voting ledger 107,227,391 at 09:56 EDT, the first voting-ledger validation of ours in its record. Fixed in 1c373c9.
- **Amendment votes.** The validator no longer votes for SingleAssetVault or LendingProtocol. Either amendment switches
  rippled's `Number` arithmetic to a 19-digit mantissa for every transaction, and the engine models the 16-digit scale
  that mainnet uses. Deployed at 10:53 EDT (below); from voting ledger 107,228,415 the validator's votes cover 46
  amendments instead of 48.
- **Validation relay.** Besides its regular peer connections, the node sends its validations over four dedicated
  connections to well-connected hub servers. Those connections read and discarded everything the hubs sent, including
  their keep-alive pings. rippled pings each peer on a 60-second timer and drops a peer that has not answered its
  previous ping by the next tick, so each relay connection closed about two minutes after it opened; on 2026-09-24 the
  four relays reconnected 87 times in one hour. These drops did not cost validations at the reference node, which
  received every non-voting validation after 21:00 through the other connections. The relays now answer the pings,
  notice a closed connection at once, and after reconnecting re-send the last validation if it went out less than 60
  seconds earlier. Fixed in 458c2c7 and deployed at 09:46; from the warm restart at 11:33 to the end of the day,
  each of the three hubs that accept peer connections held a single relay session with no drop. The fourth hub no
  longer accepts peer connections and will be replaced.
- **Soak #29 receipt and finding F401.** At ledger 107,219,029 an AMMWithdraw (withdraw all, from an XRP/DROP pool)
  came from an account holding 2.310461 XRP at an owner count of 6 with no DROP trust line; a new line at that owner
  count needs a 2.4 XRP reserve. rippled checks that reserve against the larger of the balance before the fee and the
  account's current balance, and it pays the XRP side out before it checks the DROP side, so the check saw 4.753549 XRP
  and mainnet accepted the withdrawal. The check added for F399 on 2026-09-24 compared only the balance before the fee
  and refused it. Fixed in 166f033 (deployed above).
- **Finding F402.** At ledger 107,227,378, during the new build's health check, a TicketCreate of 10 tickets paid for
  with a Ticket came from an account holding 3.398367 XRP at an owner count of 2. rippled deletes the ticket being
  spent before TicketCreate checks its reserve, so ten more tickets needed 1 + 0.2 × 11 = 3.2 XRP and mainnet
  succeeded; the engine counted the spent ticket, required 3.4 XRP and refused. The same account repeated the pattern at
  ledger 107,227,684. Fixed in e63a7d9, with the mainnet transaction pinned as a regression vector, and deployed at
  10:53: the same account's next ticket-funded TicketCreate inside the affected balance range, at ledger 107,228,620,
  matched mainnet.
- **Campaign 25 (devnet).** Forty byte-exact vectors for Batch (BatchV1_1), combined
  with trust-line and MPT clawback, AMMClawback, and MPT and IOU escrow, all already enabled on mainnet. Thirty-seven are
  byte-exact under both flow engines, with no engine finding. The other three differ only because devnet enables
  SingleAssetVault and LendingProtocol and so runs `Number` at 19 digits; libxrpl 3.4.0 under devnet's rules reproduces
  devnet's results. Merged as 677c18d.
- **fixBatchV1_2.** An amendment named fixBatchV1_2 gained majority on mainnet at 14:12 UTC. The reference node's
