- **fix(xrpl-ledger): crossing tail AMM turn is gated by strand admission (anchored-at-limit ub × trIn)** (`8f34f5c`, xrpl-validator)
  BookOfferCrossingStep::qualityUpperBound for an AMM-bearing strand with no better CLOB tip is the quality of the slice anchored AT the limit (qualityThreshold_, the inflated limitQuality), GROSSED by trIn — the book step charges the IN issuer's rate on the taker's spend even though the pool sees net amounts. When that bound misses the limit, activateNext drops the strand and the pool never fills the tail.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): the offer stream keeps reaping past the limit once the strand was built** (`60f425d`, xrpl-validator)
  rippled's offer stream has no quality gate for STEPPING: once the strand was BUILT (tip or pool within the limit at some level), rev keeps stepping past DEAD offers on levels beyond the limit — reaping them — until a LIVE offer stops it (checkQualityThreshold ends the walk at execution, not at step). Our walk broke out at the first beyond-threshold level, leaving mainnet's reaps missing.
- **fix(xrpl-ledger): walk funding prices the reserve at the maker's first-peek OwnerCount** (`cf9f395`, xrpl-validator)
  During a flow, offer deletions are DEFERRED — rippled queues crossed offers as removable (OfferCreate.cpp:460) and stale ones in ofrsToRm (StrandFlow.h:694) — so within the walk an owner's OwnerCount, hence reserve, keeps its pre-walk value for every funding check. Our sandbox deletes inline, freeing one owner-reserve unit (200000) per deletion.
