- **Soak #29 receipt and finding F401.** At ledger 107,219,029 an AMMWithdraw (withdraw all, from an XRP/DROP pool)
  came from an account holding 2.310461 XRP at an owner count of 6 with no DROP trust line; a new line at that owner
  count needs a 2.4 XRP reserve. rippled checks that reserve against the larger of the balance before the fee and the
  account's current balance, and it pays the XRP side out before it checks the DROP side, so the check saw 4.753549 XRP
  and mainnet accepted the withdrawal. The check added for F399 on 2026-09-24 compared only the balance before the fee
  and refused it. The live node was unaffected: the receipt comes from the engine comparison, and the node's ledger
  hashes kept matching. Fixed (F401) with the mainnet transaction pinned as a regression vector; the full suite (1,636
  tests) and the flow-engine suites (707 tests) pass; merged as 166f033; deployment awaits a decision.
- **Voting-ledger validations (merged, not yet deployed).** Every 256th ledger (sequence ≡ 255 mod 256) is a voting
  ledger: a validator's validation of it carries its amendment votes. The node signed those validations but placed the
  signature after the amendment list, which puts the fields out of canonical order, and rippled drops a validation whose
  fields are out of order (the check is present in rippled 3.3.0 and 3.4.0). The reference node's record from 2026-09-24
  12:48 to 2026-09-25 00:22 EDT has our validation for none of the 41 voting ledgers in that period, so the network never
  received our amendment votes; since 21:00 on 2026-09-24 it has our validation for every other ledger. The signature now
  takes its canonical place; the signed content is unchanged. Verified on mainnet with a throwaway key: the same
  voting-ledger validation, sent through a public hub, reached the reference node 93 ms later in the corrected layout
  and did not arrive in the old one. Two new tests; the full suite passes (1,635 tests). Merged as 1c373c9; it ships
  with the next deployment.
- **Validation relay (merged, not yet deployed).** Besides its regular peer connections, the node sends its validations
  over four dedicated connections to well-connected hub servers. Those connections read and discarded everything the
  hubs sent, including their keep-alive pings. rippled pings each peer on a 60-second timer and drops a peer that has
  not answered its previous ping by the next tick, so each relay connection closed about two minutes after it opened,
  and the relay noticed only when its next write failed. On 2026-09-24 the four relays reconnected 87 times in one hour.
  These drops did not cost validations at the reference node, which received every non-voting validation after 21:00
  through the other connections; the missed validations were the voting-ledger issue above. The relays now answer the
  pings, notice a closed connection at once rather than at the next write, and after reconnecting re-send the last
  validation if it went out less than 60 seconds earlier (rippled discards a validation signed more than three minutes
  before it arrives). Twelve new tests; the full suite passes (1,633 tests). Merged as 458c2c7; it ships with the next
  deployment, together with the first warm restart.
