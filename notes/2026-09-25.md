- **Validation relay (merged, not yet deployed).** Besides its regular peer connections, the node sends its validations
  over four dedicated connections to well-connected hub servers. Those connections read and discarded everything the
  hubs sent, including their keep-alive pings. rippled pings each peer on a 60-second timer and drops a peer that has
  not answered its previous ping by the next tick, so each relay connection closed about two minutes after it opened,
  and the relay noticed only when its next write failed. On 2026-09-24 the four relays reconnected 87 times in one hour,
  and each drop could lose the validation written into it. The relays now answer the pings, notice a closed connection
  at once rather than at the next write, and after reconnecting re-send the last validation if it went out less than
  60 seconds earlier (rippled discards a validation signed more than three minutes before it arrives). Twelve new tests;
  the full suite passes (1,633 tests). Merged as 458c2c7; it ships with the next deployment, together with the first
  warm restart.
