- **Finding F405 (AMM withdrawal by the token's issuer).** At ledger 107,278,773 an account withdrew all of its share
  of an AMM pool of XRP and a token the account itself issues, while holding 1.41 XRP and owning two ledger objects.
  Before paying out a token, rippled checks that a withdrawer with no trust line for it could afford the reserve for
  a new one. The check asks only whether that trust line exists, and an issuer never has a trust line to itself, so
  it applies to the issuer too: a third object needs 1 + 0.2 × 3 = 1.6 XRP, and even with the pool's whole 0.1 XRP the
  account had at most 1.50999 XRP, so mainnet refused the withdrawal (tecINSUFFICIENT_RESERVE, with the fee charged).
  The engine exempted issuers from the check, an assumption made when the check was added on 2026-09-04 that no
  mainnet transaction had tested until this one, and paid out. The exemption is removed and the mainnet transaction
  is pinned as a regression vector. Fixed in 51b0cbe on 2026-09-27; not yet deployed.
